Useful answers

Before we get started.

Who is Adaptix for?

Our current work focuses on schools and pupils through practical workshops and developing classroom materials. We are also open to conversations with community organisations and educational collaborators.

Are full curriculum packages available to buy?

Not yet. We are developing initial samples and teaching resources and welcome feedback from interested schools.

How much does a school workshop cost?

We prepare a quotation after discussing your year group, group size, location, space, equipment and learning priorities.

Can we discuss a research or educational partnership?

Yes. We welcome conversations about research, engineering education, teacher resources and community engagement.
